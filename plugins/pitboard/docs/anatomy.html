The status row in the CLI and Claude Code Desktop

One row of signals next to the prompt. Grey means nothing needs you. A colour and a number appear only once a reading crosses its threshold. Each reading has a card with its name and its readings, a click away in the CLI and a hover away in Desktop; /pitboard unfolds every card in a pane with what each reading means.

Light and dark terminal. A gauge is two cells of a block that rises in eighths (▁ to █) over a grey track; the track and a calm fill are theme colours, so both stay visible on either background. The name stands left of its gauge; a dot parts readings of one group, │ the groups. ↻ after the context: auto-compact is on. The cache gauge fills as its TTL runs out; a flame while a reply runs, a snowflake once expired. A click (or Enter under the focus) on a name opens its readings as one line with a ▲ under it, and advice under them only when the reading needs a decision; a second click closes it.

Colours

A colour means one thing everywhere: none, nothing to do (good values stay uncoloured too); amber, worth a glance; red, changes what to do next; blue, a cold cache; green, a state that is on (auto-compact). Model and effort colours name the model, not a state.

Worth knowing

  • The row shows the advisor setting. /advisor may say the running conversation keeps its previous advisor until /clear or /compact; a plugin cannot read that state.
  • A Desktop card is drawn with the row, and the row redraws only when what it shows changes, so a card's paces and times can be up to a minute old. In the last 5 minutes of the cache the row counts seconds, so a card opened then can stay up.
  • Desktop draws the band's frame and the card's popover frame itself; the plugin draws only their content.
  • With every session idle the limits do not move; the scoped readings drop off after 15 minutes and return with the next endpoint reading in the file.
